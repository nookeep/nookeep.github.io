Information and purposes

You can keep personal records in the NooKeep Android app without an account. Account, friend, and letter information is processed when you use those features.

On-device records
Photos you take or select are used to isolate subjects. Stickers, daily boxes, notes, layouts, letter drafts, and settings stay in app-specific device storage. Private friend nicknames also stay on your device and are not sent to the other person.
Accounts and friends
We use your account ID, email and basic profile supplied through Google sign-in, display name, goose avatar, friend code, requests, relationships, blocks, and rules acceptance to provide authentication and social features. We do not collect your address book.
Letters
When you send a letter, we store its finished image and thumbnail, sender and recipient, and delivery and read status. Written text and placed stickers form part of that image; the letter text is not stored separately on the server. We do not automatically upload your personal boxes or unsent drafts.
Reports and moderation
We process reporter and target account IDs, report reason, submission time, review access, and actions. A letter report includes a copy of the selected letter image. Hashes of images removed for violations help prevent the same file from being sent again.
Support
We use the contact details and content you send by email to handle your request. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or identity documents.
Abuse prevention
We use account IDs, request counts, and request times to limit excessive requests.

Providers and locations

Google ML Kit

Google ML Kit isolates subjects on your device without sending the photos to Google for that processing. For model delivery, diagnostics, and usage analysis, Google processes device and app information, device or installation identifiers, performance and error information, and processing details such as image format and size. See ML Kit’s data disclosure.

Google Firebase

Accounts, friends, and letters use Google Firebase. Firebase Authentication processes authentication data in the United States and uses IP addresses and user-agent information for authentication security and abuse prevention. Storage locations vary by Firebase service; using a database in Korea does not mean all processing takes place in Korea. See Firebase’s privacy information.

Remote diagnostics

Remote Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring collection is currently disabled by default. If it is enabled, we will first explain the data collected, purposes, retention, and international transfers, and obtain any required consent. NooKeep uses neither advertising tracking nor Google Analytics. ML Kit’s provider diagnostics described above are separate.

Sharing and access

Letters are exchanged with the recipient you choose. Removing a friend prevents new letters until you become friends again. Blocking also prevents new friend requests. Neither action removes access to letters already received. There is no sender recall feature. We do not disclose a reporter’s identity to the reported person.

Authorized reviewers use only submitted report evidence for moderation, and evidence access and actions are recorded. They are not provided a view of all private letters. Letters are not end-to-end encrypted.

When you export an image, a copy is shared with the external app you choose. That app’s policies govern its subsequent storage and deletion.

Retention and deletion

Personal records
Records stay on your device until you remove them or clear app data. Uninstalling or clearing app data can delete them. Android or device-manufacturer backup settings may create separate backup or transfer copies.
Accounts and friends
Account, profile, and rules-acceptance information is kept until account deletion. Friend relationships and blocks reflect changes you make in the app and are removed on account deletion.
Delivered letters
Delivered letters do not automatically expire. They remain after removing or blocking a friend. If the sender deletes their account or a letter is removed for a violation, its server original is removed and recipients see a deleted status.
Incomplete uploads
Uploads that are not accepted become eligible for cleanup 24 hours after reservation. Upload copies left after acceptance are also cleaned up.
Report records
To review reports, handle appeals, and prevent repeated violations, report evidence and access records are kept for 90 days from report submission, then cleaned up. Related blocked-image hashes use the report’s expiry date. This retention also applies after account deletion.
Deletion markers
A minimal completed-account-deletion marker is retained for 24 hours to support retries and reject stale requests.
Request limits
Records used to limit excessive requests become eligible for cleanup 24 hours after the last update.

Deleted data becomes inaccessible in the service, and its files and database records are cleaned up. Offline devices apply letter deletion updates when they reconnect. Copies already exported cannot be recalled.

Under Firebase’s published policy, Authentication retains logged IP addresses for a few weeks. Other authentication data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after the provider receives the account-deletion request. This is separate from NooKeep’s 24-hour deletion marker.

Your rights and contact

In the app, go to Postbox → Postbox settings → Delete account. This removes your authentication account, profile, friendships, and sent-letter originals. Letters sent by someone else may remain in their records with your identifying information anonymized. Personal boxes and stickers on your device remain separate from the account.

You can also contact dev.utact@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing without using the app. We verify only the information needed to establish account ownership and explain the outcome. See account deletion instructions.

Changes to this policy and their effective date will be published on this page.

Permissions and security

Camera access is used to take photos. Notification permission is used for reminders you allow. When selecting photos, we access the items you choose through the system picker. Declining an optional permission leaves other personal-record features available.

Server communication uses encrypted connections. User access and operator permissions are separate. We do not intentionally put letter content, photos, display names, or authentication tokens in logs. Report evidence is restricted to authorized personnel.

This website

This website contains no advertising or visitor-analytics scripts and sets no NooKeep tracking cookies. This website has no sign-in or input forms. GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, even when they are not signed in to GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s privacy statement.
