Delete your account

Updated: November 2, 2026

Choose a deletion method

Use either of the following methods.

Option 1In the app

Postbox →Postbox settings →Delete account

Option 2By email

Write email

Send your NooKeep sign-in email and account deletion request to dev.utact@gmail.com. You do not need to reinstall the app.

We will reply with verification steps and the expected completion date, then confirm deletion.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, or identity documents.

What is deleted

Account and friend information
Your authentication account, profile, friend code, friendships, and acceptance records are deleted.
Sent letters
Server originals are deleted. Recipients see a deleted status instead of the content.
Account files on your device
Completing deletion in the app also clears that account’s drafts, letter files, and private friend nicknames on that device.

What remains and exceptions

Personal boxes and stickers
These stay on your device because they are separate from your account.
Letters from other people
Originals may remain in the sender’s records. Your identifying information is anonymized.
Letters on offline devices
Deletion updates apply when the device reconnects.
Exported copies
Copies already exported cannot be recalled.
Files on your device
An email request does not immediately erase these files. You can delete personal records in the app or clear app data.
Device backups
Copies kept by Android or a device backup service must be managed through that service.

Information kept temporarily

Report records
Report evidence and access records are kept separately for 90 days from submission for review, appeals, and prevention of repeated violations.
Blocked-image hashes
Kept until the related report expires.
Account-deletion marker
Kept for 24 hours after completion.
Firebase authentication data
Excluding IP logs, authentication data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days of the provider receiving the deletion request.
Firebase Authentication IP logs
Kept for a few weeks.
