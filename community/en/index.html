Community rules

Effective: November 2, 2026

These rules apply to friends and letters. Agree to them in the app before exchanging letters. You can use personal boxes without an account.

Content you can’t send

  • Harassment and deception Harassment, hate, threats, impersonation, scams, or spam
  • Rights and privacy Content that violates someone’s rights or privacy
  • Sexual abuse and exploitation Sexual abuse or exploitation, including that of children, and related content
  • Non-consensual images Illegally recorded images or intimate images shared without consent
  • Violent or illegal content Graphic violence or other illegal content

Blocking, reporting, and moderation

Blocking prevents new friend requests and letters without notifying the other person. Letters already received remain available; unblocking does not restore the friendship.

Report a received letter from its options menu, or report a user from their friend profile. Authorized reviewers may issue a warning, restrict new sending, or remove a letter based on the selected evidence.

Files matching removed images are blocked while the related hash is retained. We do not use AI to automatically review every new image.

Child safety

We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including related content. Use the child-safety report reason in the app or the contact below. Confirmed violating content is made inaccessible in the service and reported to appropriate authorities as required by applicable law.

Contact and appeals

Contact dev.utact@gmail.com with reports, questions about restrictions, or appeals. Explain which action you are asking about and why you would like it reconsidered.

Do not attach unrelated private letters or identity documents. We do not disclose the reporter’s identity to the reported person.

Report evidence is kept for 90 days after submission for review, appeals, and prevention of repeated violations. See the privacy policy for retention details.
